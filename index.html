03 / A practical workflow

Make your product list useful.

A long list of Oopbuy finds can be difficult to compare. A smaller list with consistent information makes differences easier to see.

Start your CAFANS shortlist ↗
  1. 01

    Choose a category and a purpose

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warm layer, sportswear or an accessory. Keep each shortlist focused on comparable items.

  2. 02

    Save the original listing details

    Record the product URL, selected variant, listed currency and date checked. Keep seller statements separate from details you have independently confirmed.

  3. 03

    Check measurements and photos

    Compare garment measurements with an item you own. Where available, inspect close-up photos for stitching, hardware, labels and the selected color.

  4. 04

    Compare the full cost

    Add delivery and applicable fees before deciding. Recheck the product page and the relevant provider's terms before payment.

06 / Direct answers

Before you
open another tab.

Quick answers about the guide, comparison process and limits of product research lists.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize links and comparison notes for shopping research. Useful fields include category, listed price, size information, photos and the date checked. A list does not guarantee availability, authenticity or seller reliability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS or the corresponding CAFANS category pages.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the units and whether measurements describe the garment or the body. Letter sizes alone are not enough.

How can I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any service or payment fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges with the relevant provider before paying.

Do brand names or product photos prove authenticity?

No. Brand names, logos and listing photos are not proof of authenticity. Check the seller's identity, supporting documentation and return terms. The photographs on this guide are editorial references, not verified CAFANS inventory.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It does not represent Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
